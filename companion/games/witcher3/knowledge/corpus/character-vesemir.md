Vesemir is the oldest living witcher and the venerable sword-combat
instructor of the School of the Wolf at the keep of Kaer Morhen. He is
Geralt's mentor and something of a gruff adoptive father figure to the
witchers of his school.

In The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt he appears in the prologue at Kaer Morhen
while Ciri trains, then travels with Geralt at the start of the search
for her. He is cautious, experienced, and often the voice of restraint
compared to younger witchers.
