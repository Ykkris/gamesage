Witcher Senses are the core investigation mechanic of The Witcher 3:
Wild Hunt, activated by holding a button. While active, the world is
tinted and important traces stand out: blood, footprints, sounds,
scents, and objects of interest glow red.

Quests frequently require following these tracks to locate monsters,
people, or clues, and to examine highlights in the environment. Witcher
Senses also let Geralt eavesdrop through walls and spot items that are
otherwise easy to miss.
