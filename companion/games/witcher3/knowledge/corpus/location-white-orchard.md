White Orchard is the first open-world region of The Witcher 3: Wild
Hunt: a small farming village in Temeria under Nilfgaardian occupation
after the war. It serves as the tutorial area where the player learns
exploration, Witcher Senses, combat, alchemy, and Gwent.

Notable places in and around the village include the notice board, the
tavern, the sawmill, the Nilfgaardian garrison camp, the local smith,
and the fields and mill where the griffin attacks travelers.
