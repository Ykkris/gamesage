The Beast of White Orchard is the first main quest of The Witcher 3: Wild
Hunt after the prologue at Kaer Morhen. Geralt and Vesemir stop in the
village of White Orchard, where a griffin has been attacking travelers.

The investigation uses Witcher Senses to follow blood trails and claw
marks, includes questioning the hunter Mislav, and leads to the site
where a dead griffin — the attacker's slain mate — is examined with
Vesemir. Preparing for the fight involves brewing or buying bait and
choosing signs and oils effective against beasts.

The quest ends with the griffin fight outside the village. Completing it
leads into events at the tavern and the journey to Vizima.
