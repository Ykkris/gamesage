Lilac and Gooseberries is an early main quest of The Witcher 3: Wild
Hunt. Geralt searches the region of Velen for the sorceress Yennefer,
whose trademark scent of lilac and gooseberries identifies her trail.

The quest is a tracking investigation: questioning peasants and
Nilfgaardian soldiers, examining campsites and belongings with Witcher
Senses, and following leads toward the sorceress' destination. It also
opens the way into the larger Velen storyline around Crow's Perch and
the Bloody Baron.

Following Yennefer's trail eventually points Geralt toward Skellige as
the next step of the search.
